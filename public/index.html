How matches work

First, all course content is broken up into chunks. Then, each chunk is turned into a vector in $\mathbb{R}^{384}$ in a way that attempts to capture the meaning of each chunk. (This is why searching "orthogonal" and "perpendicular" shows you the same results.) When you type a phrase in the search bar, that phrase is also turned into a vector in $\mathbb{R}^{384}$, and matches are found by computing the cosine similarity between your search vector and the vectors representing course content. This conversion is handled by the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model on HuggingFace.

Results also use keyword matching. You can search within specific materials, for example “HW 4 problem 3”, “lecture 9 projection”, or “practice midterm 1 problem 3”.
